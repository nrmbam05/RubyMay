Girl in patterns

My aim was not to portray a strong emotion in this painting rather an appreciation of the natural shapes and colours we see in ourselves, our work and in nature. Yet somehow when I look at the subject I read her subtle emotion as a listening person.

In playing with the shapes I mimicked the arched structure of the flower with the frame around the portrait. Then contrasted the rigidity of the arch's with the flowing background and unsymmetrical flowers.

With colour I wanted to created a harmonised warm feeling with small uses of blue in the face to direct the viewers eyes to the portrait.

But in the end, while I did this painting to enjoy shape and colour, I now see it as an emotive painting with a listening subject.
