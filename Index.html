FLOWER AND FACE SERIES

Like many artists I love shapes and colour. Within this series I explored and experimented with the connection between emotion and communication with structure and colours of flowers and the face.

Society has attached flowers with such a complicated emotional meaning, it is fascinating how we give flowers both to express love, sorrow and beauty. In these paintings I contemplated ideas surrounding what it means to receive flowers and the connection between flowers and femininity/ girlhood.

Alongside flowers, I focused on portraits in light of structure and colour because I had an interest in capturing subtle moods and emotions. It is interesting how much our communication and interpretation of each other’s emotion comprises of reading slight changes in facial expressions. As a relationship develops often these subtle expressions grow into obvious cues with meaning.

However, across all of my paintings I found an odd satisfaction in combining the organic structures of flowers and faces with more obvious man-made-like patterns, arches and structures to see how they could complement each other.

about

Wellington/Whangarei based artist

Natasha is an artist based in either Northland or Wellington ( seasonal re-locator).

Born from a family of artists it seemed likely she'd follow in their footsteps. Currently her work focues on exploring ideas surrounding flowers and portraits. With an interest in adding unpexceted colours to the skintone alongside capturing the sutble emotion in her subjects expression.

Outside of her art she is a full time student studying Law and Economics.
