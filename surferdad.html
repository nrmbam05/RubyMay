Dad by me

Both of my parents spent a large portion of their 20s exploring.

In a conversation with Dad about my fears of being stuck under a wave, he compared it being stuck under the water while whitewater rafting in a crocodile infested river in Africa. Dad's adventures occasionally leaned on the side of dangerous.

This is a painting of my Dad about to go surfing in Kaikōura, luckily there weren't crocodiles
